FAQ

Common Questions

Do I really pay nothing upfront? +
Yes — absolutely nothing upfront. Our entire service (consultation, university shortlist, document preparation, application submission) is delivered before any payment. You only pay our fee of ৳50,000 after you receive a confirmed offer letter from a university.
Which countries do you cover? +
We currently cover 9 European countries: Germany, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Austria, Czech Republic, Poland, Croatia, and Malta. All have been specifically selected for their affordability, work rights, and PR pathways for Bangladeshi students.
Do I need IELTS to apply? +
It depends on the country and university. Many European universities accept a Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter from your previous institution instead of IELTS. We guide you on the exact requirements for your target universities.
Is Germany really free? What's the catch? +
Public universities in Germany charge almost no tuition (€0–500/semester). The challenge for Bangladeshi students is: (1) you need an APS Certificate from the German embassy, (2) visa appointments in Dhaka have a 12–19 month backlog, and (3) you need a blocked account (Sperrkonto) showing ~€11,200. We guide you through all of this.
Can I work part-time while studying? +
Yes — in all the countries we recommend, students are legally allowed to work 20 hours/week during term and full-time during holidays. This significantly helps with living expenses.
What is included in the ৳50,000 fee? +
As part of our Dhaka office grand opening offer, our flat fee of ৳50,000 covers everything: free consultation, personalized university shortlisting, SOP and all document preparation, application submission, and visa document support. This is a limited-time launch price — once we've placed our first cohort, visa support will return to a separate charge.
How long does the whole process take? +
For most countries: 3–6 months from consultation to offer letter, then 2–4 months for visa. Germany is an exception due to the APS certificate and visa appointment backlog — we recommend starting 18 months before your intended intake.
